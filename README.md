## 🏰 Game Overview

**The Secret Ingredient** is a medieval tavern mamangement and survival game with a twist.

By **day**, you play as a hardworking tavern owner serving drinks to customers.  
But these aren’t ordinary drinks — they’re brewed using a mysterious **special ingredient**.

By **night**, the tavern closes… and the real work begins.

You descend into the basement to battle slimes and harvest the special ingredient needed to craft more drinks for the next day.

The gameplay loop revolves around:

- 🗡 Slaying slimes at night
- 🧪 Harvesting special ingredients
- 🍻 Crafting and selling drinks by day
- 💰 Maximizing profit
- 🌙 Surviving as many days as possible

The better you fight, the more you brew.  
The more you brew, the richer you get.  
But if you fail to survive the night… the tavern falls.

---

## 🔁 Core Gameplay Loop

1. **Day Phase**
   - Serve customers drinks
   - Earn money
   - Manage resources

2. **Night Phase**
   - Enter combat mode
   - Defeat slimes
   - Harvest special ingredients
   - Survive until morning

The goal:  
**Earn as much money as possible and survive as many days as you can.**
